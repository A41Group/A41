It started with
a question in class.

While pursuing my advanced diploma in Supply Chain Management at George Brown Polytechnic, I learned about institutions purchasing collectively. I wanted to explore what that could mean for everyday Canadians.

Read the founder’s story

As an everyday Canadian, I think often about the cost of living and the opportunities available to us. During my studies, I learned how health and education institutions use group purchasing organizations to bring their purchasing needs together. That raised a question: could the same thinking help everyday households?

A41 grew out of that question and my ambition to build a sustainable social enterprise. By developing relationships with Canadian companies, organizing procurement, and connecting people through a focused catalogue, we’re working toward more affordable food and stronger Canadian supply chains. It starts small, with people choosing to build something together.
